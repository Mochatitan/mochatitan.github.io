Brand Guide

Brand Concept

Anytutor is an online tutoring platform dedicated to tutoring children K-12 in mathematics, all of it will be done virtually, and is on-demand rather than most tutoring websites where you need to schedule. You pay a subscription fee, and while you are subscribed you may join a live tutoring room where you can get help using online calling and a shared whiteboard that lets them explain things to you.

Color Scheme

Dark Forest Green

The primary iconic color, used in logos, header background, and promotionals


Light Gray

Used for readable elements and backgrounds, as to not jar the webpage viewer with too strong colors we have the text elements in a more neutral color


Dark Gray

Used in places to complement with the dark green, giving a calm dark-themed vibe, used for backgrounds, the header, and will pair with the dark green often in things like promotionals


Fonts [written in their font]

Verdana is used for the header and title

Georgia is used for the Navigation bar at the top

Times New Roman is used for the body

Slogan

Anytime, Anywhere, Anylevel, Anytutor.
